Add `<lu-mascot>` (`@slashome/lumen/mascot`), the mascot of the application: by default the Lumen fairy, a winged light drawn in the theme colors, with an `empty` variant in dotted outline. An application draws its own with `registerMascot()`. Decorative without `label`, an image named by its `label` with one. New semantic token `--lu-color-glow`, the bright heart of a light.
